Method

What this model is, what it takes in, what it gives back — and, in the same breath, what it does not contain.

What the model is
  • A deterministic cohort simulation over 60 months. No randomness, no probability, no fitted parameters.
  • Company ARR is only ever the sum of its cohorts. Aggregates are never asserted; they are summed.
  • The same engine runs the browser and the command-line checks — one source of truth, 35 integrity checks.
What is input
  • Management decisions — S&M, and the fixed operating costs R&D and G&A.
  • Economic transition assumptions — persistence and expansion (how the installed base evolves), CAC per €1 of New ARR (how spend becomes ARR), gross margin (how revenue becomes gross profit).
  • Nothing else. There is no plan, no target, no forecast to hit.
What is output
  • ARR and its cohort composition, revenue, gross profit, EBITA, FCF, cash.
  • R12M GRR, expansion and NRR — measured, never entered.
  • CAC payback, an output of CAC/ARR × 12 ÷ GM. In the first version it was an input; inverting that was the most important correction this project has made.
Reporting basis — MRR ⇄ ARR
  • SaaS Physics stores recurring state internally in MRR. ARR is an exact 12× reporting view (ARR = 12 × MRR). MRR and ARR are two reporting bases for the same recurring economic state; switching basis changes no economic result.
  • MRR/ARR are recurring run-rate state measures. Revenue, gross profit and FCF are period financial flows and therefore do not toggle — they stay labelled · this month in both bases.
  • The default is MRR: the engine steps monthly, and New/Expansion/Leakage are monthly state movements, so a monthly basis matches the simulation's own resolution.
Reality vs measurement
  • The business exists first. KPIs are measurements taken from it — a KPI definition must never become an economic law.
  • The engine\u2019s coefficients are not the KPIs of similar name. A 90% persistence coefficient measures as 89.56% R12M GRR, and that gap is the within-period interaction of decay and expansion, not an error.
  • Two layers, kept apart: the economic engine, and the measurement layer that observes it.
Homogeneous Control World
  • The default has flat transition laws: age carries no differential dynamics.
  • Under homogeneous laws, cohort vintage affects provenance and capital history but does not create differential forward ARR dynamics.
  • It reduces exactly to ARR(t+1) = g\u00b7ARR(t) + N, verified to 4\u00d710\u207b\u00b9\u2075 against the engine.
  • This is a control case, not a claim that real retention is flat.
State-dependent illustrative world
  • Used only where explicitly selected — Scenario 6 and nowhere else.
  • It assigns different persistence and expansion to three age bands. That profile is a user assumption about a world, never a claimed law of SaaS.
  • It exists to show what can occur if transition behaviour varies with cohort maturity. It is not evidence that it does.
Major current boundaries
  • #10 Acquisition is linear and unbounded at the default (saturation spend off). Set a finite saturation spend on the rail to bound New ARR — that is the mechanism that can say stop. No canonical "spend more" scenario is shipped; demo it on the slider. S&M itself is unconstrained in cash unless a cash reserve is set.
  • #12 / #19 Age bands default flat. Tenure laws on the rail write the engine’s existing three-band array; Scenario 6 keeps its shared construction. The default opening book is still one age-0 €20m cohort.
  • #14 Expansion is free at the default (expansion CAC = 0). Set Expansion CAC / €1 Exp ARR to price expansion without changing the ARR path.
  • #15 FCF = EBITA at the default (prepaid term off). Set a prepaid term so FCF = EBITA + N × ΔMRR — growth is then cash-generative at the WC line. No tax, capex or debt.
  • #16 R&D has no modelled effect on retention or expansion — which is why it is a fixed cost here, not a lever.
  • #18 Leakage is one number at the default (logo retention off), so churn and contraction cannot be separated. Set logo retention to split leakage into logo churn and contraction — ARR does not move.
  • #21 There is no price.
What each visual earns its place by
  • Cohort strata \u2014 the historical composition and provenance of today\u2019s recurring state.
  • Capital-recovery track \u2014 the elapsed time between acquisition investment and economic recovery, so CAC payback becomes a distance rather than a number.
  • System map \u2014 a model audit: the causal topology the engine contains, with \u2298 marking links it does not.
  • Cohorts \u2014 composition + vintage reading + age toggle. Which vintage carries or leaks the book, and whether blended NRR conceals variation. Not a triangle and not a hero retention number.
  • Appendix \u2014 a month \u00d7 KPI table of the current run, with a compact M(t−1) → flows → M(t) month audit. Companion to System; does not replace Method, System, or Scenarios 5 & 6.
Illustrative company throughout. No real company data is connected, and no valuation, multiple or embedded value is computed. The engine is deliberately a portable synthetic methodology: translating a real company into an opening state would be the job of a separate adapter, which does not exist and is out of scope.
