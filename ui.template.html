What this model does and does not claim

Stated v0.1 equations

monthly GRR = annual GRR ^ (1/12) monthly expansion = (1 + annual) ^ (1/12) − 1 retained = opening × monthly GRR leakage = opening − retained expansion = retained × monthly expansion closing = retained + expansion (+ new cohort) New ARR = S&M ÷ (CAC / New ARR) ← v0.2 / v0.4 default (k off) New ARR = (k / CAC) × S&M / (S&M + k) ← v0.4 when k is set payback = (CAC / New ARR) × 12 ÷ GM ← OUTPUT (stated CAC) revenue = ((opening + closing) / 2) / 12 EBITA = GP − S&M − R&D − G&A FCF = EBITA

Explicit simplifications

  • FCF = EBITA. No working capital, deferred revenue, tax, capex or debt. Real SaaS bills ahead of revenue, so cash here is pessimistic for fast growers.
  • Leakage combines churn and contraction, so the KPI layer cannot report them separately however correct its definitions are. Reactivation = 0. There are no customers, so logo retention and concentration do not exist.
  • Retention and expansion rates do not vary with cohort age, segment or product.
  • The future economic benefit of R&D is outside the simulation boundary. v0.2 deliberately encodes no R&D → retention or R&D → expansion law; there is no defensible universal coefficient. A later version may let you state a hypothesised delayed effect as an explicit management thesis.
  • Acquisition is instantaneous in S&M — no lag. Saturation exists (v0.4) but is off by default, so the shipped world is still linear.
  • Revenue uses a midpoint approximation of a stock that actually moves continuously.

What v0.2 changed, and what it did not

  • Fixed. Gross margin no longer generates ARR. In v0.1, cutting GM 80%→65% wrongly raised New ARR €0.75m→€0.92m/month; now it leaves New ARR untouched and moves CAC payback 18.0→22.2 months instead.
  • Still true. Improving acquisition efficiency and raising S&M by the same factor produce an identical ARR path — only cash separates them (€27m over 60 months at the calibrated settings).
  • v0.4 — optional bound. A finite saturation spend k makes New ARR = (k/CAC)×S&M/(S&M+k). The default k is null, so the linear generator is reproduced exactly.
  • Discovered (v0.2). Retention-heavy and expansion-heavy businesses at matched NRR are economically indistinguishable to this model. A boundary, not a bug — see docs/MATCHED-NRR.md.
  • v0.2.1 — two layers. The economic engine holds transition coefficients; the KPI engine holds measurement definitions, and events flow one way between them. A 90% persistence coefficient measures as 89.56% R12M GRR: the whole gap is the within-period interaction of decay and expansion, and it vanishes if either is switched off. See docs/MEASUREMENT.md.
  • v0.2.1 — sharper boundary. Measured R12M NRR is a stock ratio, so pinning it pins the monthly multiplier uniquely. Any two systems reporting the same NRR are identical in this model by construction, not by coincidence.
