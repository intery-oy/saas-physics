What you are looking at

Every visual property below is bound to a number the simulation actually computes. Nothing moves decoratively.

The mass
  • Height = ARR. The recurring stock, at every moment of its life.
  • Strata = cohorts. Each band is one acquisition vintage; the opening base is the bottom layer and every month deposits a new one on top, so vertical position is age.
  • Colour runs oldest–darkest to newest–lightest, so a vintage stays traceable for its whole life.
  • Brightness = gross margin. Same mass, dimmer, means the same recurring revenue carrying less economic content.
  • A brighter top edge marks a cohort in its first two months — new business entering.
Below the line
  • The shadow = cumulative leakage. Every euro the installed base has lost and had to replace. It only ever deepens.
  • Deliberately calm slate, not red: leakage is physics, not an alarm.
  • The keel = cash. Above the seam is cash generated, below it is cash consumed.
Time
  • Left of the playhead is realised: solid, filled, already true.
  • Right of the playhead is projected: unfilled outline, because it has not happened.
  • The playhead is now. Pause anywhere and the right-hand column describes the company at that instant.
Two futures
  • Solid, warm = Experiment, the scenario you steer.
  • Dashed, cool = Base, the frozen reference. It never changes.
  • When the assumptions match, the dashed line lies exactly on the mass. Move a force and the mass pulls away from its own ghost — the future itself has changed.
