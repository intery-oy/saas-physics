What this model does and does not claim

Stated v0.1 equations

monthly GRR = annual GRR ^ (1/12) monthly expansion = (1 + annual) ^ (1/12) − 1 retained = opening × monthly GRR leakage = opening − retained expansion = retained × monthly expansion closing = retained + expansion (+ new cohort) New ARR = S&M ÷ (CAC / New ARR) ← v0.2 payback = (CAC / New ARR) × 12 ÷ GM ← OUTPUT revenue = ((opening + closing) / 2) / 12 EBITA = GP − S&M − R&D − G&A FCF = EBITA

Explicit simplifications

  • FCF = EBITA. No working capital, deferred revenue, tax, capex or debt. Real SaaS bills ahead of revenue, so cash here is pessimistic for fast growers.
  • Leakage combines churn and contraction. Reactivation = 0.
  • Retention and expansion rates do not vary with cohort age, segment or product.
  • The future economic benefit of R&D is outside the simulation boundary. v0.2 deliberately encodes no R&D → retention or R&D → expansion law; there is no defensible universal coefficient. A later version may let you state a hypothesised delayed effect as an explicit management thesis.
  • Acquisition is linear and instantaneous in S&M — no saturation, no lag.
  • Revenue uses a midpoint approximation of a stock that actually moves continuously.

What v0.2 changed, and what it did not

  • Fixed. Gross margin no longer generates ARR. In v0.1, cutting GM 80%→65% wrongly raised New ARR €0.75m→€0.92m/month; now it leaves New ARR untouched and moves CAC payback 18.0→22.2 months instead.
  • Still true. Improving acquisition efficiency and raising S&M by the same factor produce an identical ARR path — only cash separates them (€27m over 60 months at the calibrated settings).
  • Still true. Acquisition remains linear, instantaneous and unbounded in S&M. There is no spend level at which the next euro buys less.
  • Discovered. Retention-heavy and expansion-heavy businesses at matched NRR are economically indistinguishable to this model. That is a boundary, not a bug — see docs/MATCHED-NRR.md.
