What this model does and does not claim

Stated v0.1 equations

monthly GRR = annual GRR ^ (1/12) monthly expansion = (1 + annual) ^ (1/12) − 1 retained = opening × monthly GRR leakage = opening − retained expansion = retained × monthly expansion closing = retained + expansion (+ new cohort) New ARR = S&M × 12 ÷ (payback × GM) revenue = ((opening + closing) / 2) / 12 EBITA = GP − S&M − R&D − G&A FCF = EBITA

Explicit simplifications

  • FCF = EBITA. No working capital, deferred revenue, tax, capex or debt. Real SaaS bills ahead of revenue, so cash here is pessimistic for fast growers.
  • Leakage combines churn and contraction. Reactivation = 0.
  • Retention and expansion rates do not vary with cohort age, segment or product.
  • R&D has no modelled benefit, so the model can only ever conclude "spend less on R&D". Do not take that conclusion.
  • Acquisition is linear and instantaneous in S&M — no saturation, no lag.
  • Revenue uses a midpoint approximation of a stock that actually moves continuously.

Known coupling to watch

  • Gross margin sits in the denominator of the acquisition formula, because CAC payback is defined on gross profit. Cutting GM while holding payback fixed therefore raises New ARR — the model quietly assumes acquisition got cheaper.
  • Improving CAC payback and increasing S&M by the same factor produce an identical ARR path; only the cash path separates them.
  • Both are v0.1 artefacts, implemented faithfully rather than patched. See docs/FINDINGS.md.
