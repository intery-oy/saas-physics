# SaaS Physics — Usage Log

**Six weeks. No development.**

The purpose of this log is to find out whether SaaS Physics changes real
decisions, or only produces the feeling of understanding. Those are different
things and only usage can tell them apart.

## Rules

1. **Record actual questions only.** Not demos, not "let me see what it does".
   A real CFO question you were going to have to answer anyway.
2. **Do not rebuild the engine to solve a gap.** If the instrument cannot help,
   that is the most valuable entry in this file. Write it in the *Missing
   capability* column and move on.
3. **Fill it in by hand.** No automation. The friction is the point — if it is
   not worth thirty seconds to record, it was not a real use.
4. **Record the honest outcome**, including `IRRELEVANT / COULD NOT HELP`. A log
   of only successes is a log of nothing.
5. **Write down what you believed first.** An entry without a prior belief cannot
   show whether the instrument changed anything.

## Outcome codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `CHANGED THE ANSWER` | The decision went a different way because of what this showed. |
| `SHARPENED THE ANSWER` | Same decision, but better reasoned, better bounded, or better explained to someone else. |
| `CONFIRMED THE ANSWER` | Belief held. Useful, but the weakest form of value — a model that only ever confirms is decoration. |
| `IRRELEVANT / COULD NOT HELP` | The question was outside what the engine models, or the instrument was too slow/awkward to reach for. |

## Candidate occasions

- customer economics discussion
- compensation design
- strategic planning

## After six weeks

The log, **not enthusiasm**, decides the next direction.

**Option A — Synthetic CFO laboratory.** Choose this if SaaS Physics mainly
creates intuition and improves economic reasoning without repeatedly supporting
live decisions. This is a valid and honourable outcome, and it is the more
likely one.

**Option B — Real-company decision system.** Only if repeated usage shows
material decision value. Even then, real-data integration is **gated by
physics**: acquisition is currently linear and unbounded, so the model could be
used to justify unlimited linear growth spend. That must be addressed before any
real company data goes near it.

**Do not proceed to real-company integration because the visualisation is
compelling.** That is the specific failure mode this log exists to prevent.

### Reading the log honestly

A useful prior on what the entries will probably look like: mostly
`SHARPENED` and `CONFIRMED`, with `CHANGED THE ANSWER` rare. That pattern points
to **Option A**, and finding it is a real result, not a disappointment. Option B
needs repeated `CHANGED THE ANSWER` entries on decisions that mattered — and if
the *Missing capability* column keeps naming the same gap, that gap, not the
product surface, is the next piece of work.
