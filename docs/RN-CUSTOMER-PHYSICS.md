# Research Note — Customer Physics (v2 Gate A)

Every figure below is printed by `node v2-study.js` §A and asserted by `node v2-checks.js`
(ALL-NULL-V13, A-ON, A-RECONCILE, A-NO-DUPLICATION, A-DISTINCT, A-MATCHED-WORLD,
A-ARPA-DERIVED, A-NOT-READ, A-PROVENANCE, A-COMPOSE, A-VALIDATION, A-DETERMINISM, A-MODULE)
and on screen by `node v2-accept.js`.

## Question

*How much of the loss is customers leaving, and how much is customers staying and paying less
— and can two companies with identical ARR, GRR and NRR differ in that?*

Through v1.3 the engine had no customers. Leakage was one number, persistence one asserted
coefficient (FINDINGS #18), so the question could not be posed, and the product's own R12M
panel had to say "cannot yet say how much of the loss was lost logos versus shrinking accounts".

## New economic object

A cohort now carries a customer count `n` (continuous, not integer — this is an aggregate cohort
model, not a simulation of accounts) beside its MRR. Two flat laws and one policy input:

| Input | Meaning | Null |
|---|---|---|
| `logoRetentionAnnual` (L) | 12-month share of a cohort's customers that stay | `null` = layer off |
| `contractionAnnual` (C) | 12-month revenue shrink among surviving customers | 0 |
| `newLogoARPA` | € ARR each acquired customer brings | `null` = the opening ARPA |
| `start.openingCustomers` | customers in the opening base (1,000 → ARPA €20,000) | read only when on |

With the layer on, a cohort's month is generated by `customers.js`:

```
l  = L^(1/12)          cM = 1 − (1 − C)^(1/12)          e = (1 + X)^(1/12) − 1
customers     n₁ = n₀ × l
logo churn    openingMRR × (1 − l)          departing customers take the cohort's average revenue
survivors     openingMRR × l
contraction   survivors × cM                 customers who stay and pay less
retained      survivors − contraction = openingMRR × l(1 − cM)
expansion     retained × e                   customers who stay and pay more
closing       retained + expansion
```

so `leakage = logo churn + contraction = openingMRR × (1 − l(1 − cM))`, and the v1.3 identity
`closing = opening × g × (1 + e)` holds with **`g = l(1 − cM)`: dollar persistence is DERIVED,
`P = L(1 − C)`, and `persistenceAnnual` is not read while the layer is on** (A-NOT-READ:
0.90 → 0.50 changes no month field). The engine reports `derived.persistenceAnnualEffective`,
`derived.persistenceSource`, and the persistence the input would have been.

**Where each quantity lives.** Company customers = Σ cohort customers, ARPA = ARR ÷ customers
at every cohort and every month — readouts, never stocks (A-ARPA-DERIVED). Each month record
carries `customers { opening, closing, newCustomers, logoChurn, logoChurnARR, contractionARR,
arpaOpening, arpaClosing, cumulative }` and each cohort row a `customers` sub-record; all `null`
when the layer is off.

**Provenance.** A new cohort's customers are `Σ entry.newARR ÷ entry.newLogoARPAAtSpend`, the
ARR-per-logo **stamped on the pending entry at spend** — `realiseCohort` still takes no
assumption object, and a synthetic entry with no engine state yields 400,000 ÷ 8,000 = 50
customers (A-PROVENANCE). Under a lag no customer exists before its cohort matures.

**Boundary at the engine.** L ∉ (0, 1], C ∉ [0, 1), ARR-per-logo ≤ 0, an opening vintage
without `customers`, and age bands combined with the layer all throw `RangeError`
(A-VALIDATION). The customer laws are flat; the v0.3 bands remain the ARR-only path's mechanism.

## Null world

`logoRetentionAnnual: null` (the default). The engine takes the v1.3 branch line for line.
ALL-NULL-V13 replays the complete frozen v1.3 state — every month field, every cohort scalar and
row, R12M / acquisition / expansion-cost measures at every T, summary, derived, bands, the
acquisition ledger, pending at horizon and portfolio capital, for twelve worlds including every
v1.3 mechanism on — against `baseline-v1.3-full.json.gz` (generated from the engine at
`44f7652`): **737,109 fields, worst |Δ| €0.00e+0, every world**, and every v2-only field at
its null value.

## Invariants

If the layer is correctly isolated:

- every cohort row reconciles through customer flows: opening + new − logo churn − contraction
  + expansion = closing; leakage = logo churn + contraction; customers opening + new − churned =
  closing; opening(t) = closing(t − 1) — 1,890 rows, worst residual €0.00e+0 (A-RECONCILE);
- the company record is Σ cohort sub-records, ARPA × customers = ARR, and the v1.3 ARR bridge
  still closes every month — worst residual €7.5e-9 (A-RECONCILE, A-NO-DUPLICATION);
- no customer is created or lost outside the flows: 1,000 + Σ new − Σ churned = M60 customers;
  Σ cohort initial customers = 1,000 + Σ new (A-NO-DUPLICATION);
- ARR-per-logo moves customers and ARPA and **not one euro** of ARR, revenue or cash
  (A-ARPA-DERIVED: €10,000 → twice the customers per cohort, max |ΔARR| €0.0e+0);
- the expansion cost, capacity and lag compose with the layer unchanged: Σ cohort cost = company
  line, deployed = realised + pending = Σ S&M (A-COMPOSE);
- two runs are identical in every field (A-DETERMINISM); the module keeps no state (A-MODULE).

## Falsification experiment

Run four otherwise-identical worlds with the same dollar persistence 87.4%: V (v1.3, persistence
set), B (L 87.4%, C 0 — every lost euro is a lost logo), X (L 92%, C 5%), Y (L 98%, C 10.8%).
The layer is wrong if ARR, cash, GRR or NRR differ between them; it is *empty* if the customer
state does not.

## Result

| world | P in force | max \|ΔARR\| vs V | max \|Δcash\| | M60 ARR | M60 cash |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V · v1.3, persistence set | 87.40% | — | — | €57.34m | €50.13m |
| B · every lost euro is a lost logo | 87.40% | €0.0e+0 | €0.0e+0 | €57.34m | €50.13m |
| X · fewer logos lost, survivors shrink | 87.40% | €4.5e-8 | €4.5e-8 | €57.34m | €50.13m |
| Y · almost no logos lost, heavy contraction | 87.40% | €1.4e-7 | €2.3e-7 | €57.34m | €50.13m |

The ARR-only measurement reports the same figures for all four at every T (T = 36: GRR 86.85%,
expansion 9.29%, NRR 96.14%). What the layer adds:

| world | logo retention R12M | GRR R12M | lost-logo € | contraction € | customers M60 | ARPA M60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B | 87.4% | 86.85% | 13.15% | 0.00% | 2,157 | €26,590 |
| X | 92.0% | 86.85% | 8.16% | 4.99% | 2,505 | €22,888 |
| Y | 98.0% | 86.85% | 1.98% | 11.17% | 3,046 | €18,826 |

Between B and Y the company ends with 1.41× the customers at 71% of the ARPA, on identical ARR,
revenue, cash, GRR and NRR at every month. Cumulative leakage is identical (€26.10m) and
composed oppositely: B €26.10m lost logos + €0 contraction; Y €3.93m + €22.16m. **This is a
result the v1.3 world cannot produce** — it has no object that differs between B and Y — and
it is the one the layer was built to make (A-MATCHED-WORLD, Scenario 10 in the product).

Two measurement facts follow. R12M logo retention reads back L exactly (92.000%) because it
counts customers on the frozen eligible cohort, whereas GRR does not read back anything: it is
measured in euros on a base expansion has moved, so even with C = 0 the ARR-only measurement
cannot recover L (A-DISTINCT). And the R12M GRR decomposition now exists — 100% − 8.16%
(departing customers) − 4.99% (surviving customers shrinking) = 86.85%, identity residual
0.0e+0 — which is what FINDINGS #18 asked for.

## Boundary

- **One ARPA per cohort.** Customers within a cohort are homogeneous; departing customers take
  exactly the cohort's average revenue. No size heterogeneity, no concentration, no "the big
  ones leave first". Recorded as the Gate A boundary; a heterogeneity mechanism would be its
  own named object.
- **Flat laws.** L and C do not vary with cohort age; the v0.3 age bands cannot be combined with
  the layer (rejected, not silently merged).
- **No reactivation, no downgrade-then-recover, no customer-level expansion distribution.**
  Expansion is still the generic survivor coefficient until Monetization Physics (Gate B)
  replaces it with price, usage and adoption.
- **Customers are continuous.** 37.5 customers a month is a rate, not a headcount. Integer
  cohorts would be fake precision at this level of abstraction.
